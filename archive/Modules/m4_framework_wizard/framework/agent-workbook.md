## The 72 Key Points (Onboarding Rubric)
These are the key points a Regenerative Neighborhood needs to consider, plan, and share effectively.

---

## Section 0: Basic Info

1. **Living options offered** (select multiple)
   - Regenerative neighborhood experience (short/mid-term rentals, co.living)
   - Regenerative Neighborhoods (buy/steward land, long-term/permanent)

2. **Status** - Stage of neighborhood functionality
   - Plan only, no land yet
   - Land acquired, no construction
   - Under construction, no one living
   - Under construction, community living
   - Most housing built, receiving new members
   - Mostly built, wait list

3. **Name** - Neighborhood name
4. **Country**
5. **State**
6. **City**
7. **Address** (or relative location)
8. **Land Size** (acres or hectares)
9. **Timeline** - Year started planning / acquired land / started building / moved in

## Section 10: Community Culture

**Personal Foundations:**

58. **Your Values** *(What matters to you — the qualities you find meaningful and worth pursuing)*
    - What are your top 3–5 core VALUES? (e.g., authenticity, freedom, service, creativity, sustainability)
    - Why does each one matter to you?

59. **Your Principles** *(Your operating rules — the guidelines you follow when making decisions)*
    - What are your top 3–5 PRINCIPLES? (e.g., "always tell the truth," "first understand then be understood," "build before you sell")
    - How do these show up in how you live and work?

60. **Your Rules** *(Your non-negotiable boundaries — the lines you will not cross)*
    - What are your top 3–5 RULES? (e.g., "no gossip," "complete what you start," "respect every living thing")
    - What happens when a rule is broken?
