# Regenerative Neighborhood Framework Wizard — How It Works

A guide for new users, coaches, and founding teams.

---

## What this tool is

The Wizard is a guided self-assessment for people building intentional communities and regenerative neighborhoods. It walks a founding team through four phases of development — from initial spark to living, operating community — asking the questions that matter at each stage.

The output is twofold: a **live diagnostic radar** showing where your project is strong and where it needs work, and a **project document** you can export and share with your team, investors, or coaches.

It is not a form to fill in once and forget. It is designed to be returned to — as your project evolves, your answers deepen, and your scores move.

---

## The four frameworks, woven together

Every question in the wizard is grounded in one or more of four research-backed frameworks. Each one brings a different lens:

| Framework | Focus | What it contributes |
|---|---|---|
| **RNF** — Regenerative Neighborhood Framework | The five pillars of a healthy regenerative community | The scoring categories: Ecology, Social, Economy, Hardware, Governance |
| **Alchemy** — Community Alchemy Playbook | The human and cultural design of intentional communities | Deep questions on values, agreements, culture, activation, and lifecycle |
| **CLIPS** — Community Layers in Practice | The coherence between intention, structure, and daily practice | Checks whether what you say you believe matches what you actually do |
| **RCOS** — Regenerative Community Operating System | The six operational layers of a running community | Questions on roles, decisions, feedback loops, and learning systems |

You don't need to understand all four frameworks to use the wizard. They surface through the questions and the **Guide buttons** — small "?" buttons that appear next to every question to explain where it comes from and why it matters.

---

## The five spiral phases

The wizard is structured around five developmental spirals, grouped into four phases:

```
Phase 1 — SPARK     Spiral 1: Emergence
                    Who are you? Why this? What do you believe?

Phase 2 — PROVE     Spiral 2: Roots
                    Site, business model, funding, feasibility.

Phase 3 — BUILD     Spiral 3: Structure → Spiral 4: Activation
                    Master plan, infrastructure, capital, community culture.

Phase 4 — LIVE      Spiral 5: Continuous Regeneration
                    Operations, governance, learning, evolution.
```

Each phase ends with a **Gate** — a checkpoint that reviews whether you have the foundations in place to move forward. Gates don't block progress; they surface gaps honestly.

## Navigating the wizard

### Sidebar (left)
The left sidebar is your map. It shows every step in every phase. Click any step to jump directly to it. Steps turn bold when you're on them; they gain a checkmark when you've started answering. The active phase header is highlighted.

On mobile, tap the **Steps** tab at the bottom to show the sidebar.

### Main area (center)
This is where you answer questions. Each step contains some mix of:
- **Reflect & record** fields — open-ended text areas for your thinking
- **Checklist** — items to tick off with notes
- **Self-assessment sliders** — 0–10 scores for each dimension

Use the **← Prev** and **Next →** buttons at the bottom of each step to move through the wizard in order.

### Live diagnostic (right rail)
The right rail shows your current scores at all times:
- **Radar chart** — pentagon showing balance across the five RNF pillars
- **Pillar bars** — numerical scores with links to the steps that affect each one
- **Spiral timeline** — shows which spiral you're currently in

On mobile, tap the **Radar** tab at the bottom.

## Question types in detail

### Text fields
Open-ended questions for your team's actual thinking. There are no right answers — write what is true for your project right now. Answers are auto-saved as you type.

After each text field, use the **three status buttons** to mark where you are:

| Button          | Meaning                                 |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| ⬤ No answer yet | Skipping this for now — will return     |
| ⬤ Almost there  | Started but uncertain; flag for support |
| ⬤ Ready to go   | Answer feels solid; this one is done    |

**The "Almost there" flag is important.** It tells coaches and support from the Regen Tribe team exactly which questions you're stuck on, so they can give targeted help rather than reviewing everything.

### Checklists
Binary tick items (done / not done) with a notes area. Tick an item when it is genuinely complete — not when you plan to do it. Add notes to record evidence, decisions, or how you've approached it.

Checklist progress is shown in the section header: "3 / 7 complete."

### Self-assessment sliders
Drag from 0 to 10 to score your current state on a specific dimension. The labels "Not yet" and "Solid" anchor the ends. These scores feed directly into the radar chart.

Be honest, not aspirational. A lower score honestly given is more useful than a high score that masks a gap.

## Guide buttons

Every question has a **? Guide** button next to it. Click it to open a plain-language explainer:

- **What the question is really asking**
- **Why it matters** (which framework, which principle)
- **Examples and context** to think with
- **Common mistakes** to avoid

The guide content comes from the Community Alchemy Playbook, RCOS, CLIPS, and the RNF. You don't need to read those documents separately — the relevant guidance surfaces exactly when you need it.

Close the guide popover by clicking anywhere outside it or pressing Escape.

## The AI import feature

If your founding team already has documents — a charter, a vision deck, a business plan, meeting notes, even a long email thread — the wizard can read them and propose answers.

Click **✨ Import from document** in the right rail (or on the welcome screen). A modal will open where you can:

1. Paste your document text (or a portion of it)
2. Choose whether to fill the whole wizard or just a specific section
3. Click **Scan document**

The AI reads your text and proposes values for every relevant field — text answers, slider scores, and checklist notes. You review each proposed value before anything is saved. Nothing is applied without your confirmation.

The AI uses **MiniMax-M2.7** running via a secure backend proxy — your document content is sent to the AI but is not stored.

## The gates

At the end of phases 2 and 3, you encounter **Gate checkpoints**. A gate reviews a set of criteria — typically slider scores and checklist items — and shows you how many you've met.

- **Ready (80%+ met)** — Green. You have strong foundations to move forward.
- **Warning (50–79% met)** — Amber. You can proceed but should address gaps.
- **Not ready (<50% met)** — Red. Significant work needed before the next phase.

Gates are advisory, not locks. You can click past them. But treat a red gate as a real signal — the frameworks are pointing at something that tends to break communities if unaddressed.

## The dashboard

Click **📊 Dashboard** at the bottom of the sidebar to see an overview of your entire project in one view: all five pillar scores, the full radar, a summary of answered vs. unanswered steps, and quick links to jump to any step.

Hover over a pillar score in the right rail to see which specific steps most affect that score — and click any of them to jump there directly.

---

## Exporting your project document

Click **📄 Project document** in the right rail to open a full formatted document of everything you've filled in.

From the document view you can:
- **Download Markdown** — exports a `.md` file with all your answers, formatted with headers, tables, and checkboxes. Use it in Notion, Obsidian, or any markdown editor.
- **Print / Save as PDF** — opens your browser's print dialog. Choose "Save as PDF" for a shareable PDF. The print layout removes all UI chrome and renders cleanly on paper.

The document includes your project name, all phases and steps, every answer, all slider scores, checklist results, and gate outcomes. It ends with:

> *Generated [date] · Regenerative Neighborhood Framework v2.0 · Open Sourced by The Regen Tribe Collective =]*

## Saving and resuming

Everything is saved automatically as you type — no save button. Your progress is stored in your browser's local storage under the key `rnf_wizard_v1`.

To resume, simply return to the same URL in the same browser. Your answers and your current position in the wizard will be restored.

**To reset** and start fresh: click **Reset progress** in the right rail. This cannot be undone.

**Limitation:** Data is browser-specific. If you switch browsers or devices, your answers won't carry over. Use the Markdown export to save a snapshot before switching devices.

## Light and dark mode

Click the ☀ / ☾ toggle in the top-left of the sidebar to switch between light and dark themes. Your preference is saved automatically.

## For coaches and support staff

When a community has flagged questions as **"Almost there"**, those questions represent the exact points where a coaching conversation will have the highest leverage. Before a session:

1. Ask the community to export their Markdown document
2. Search for `status: Almost there` entries — these are the flagged fields
3. Focus your conversation there first

The wizard is designed so that a coach reviewing a community's exported document gets a complete picture of where they are, without needing to be in the same browser session.

## Technical notes

- **Stack**: React 18 (CDN) + Babel JSX transpiled in-browser, Vercel static hosting + Node.js serverless function for AI proxy
- **No backend or database** — all data lives in the user's browser localStorage
- **AI**: MiniMax-M2.7 via `/api/claude` serverless endpoint
- **Frameworks file**: `data.js` contains the complete step and field definitions — editable without touching UI code
- **Multi-file architecture**: `app.jsx` (shell), `views.jsx` (step renderer + welcome + dashboard), `components.jsx` (shared UI), `document.jsx` (export view), `import.jsx` (AI modal), `styles.css` (all styles)
